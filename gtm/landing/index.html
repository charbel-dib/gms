What if the internet cuts at the gym?

GMS works from any phone on 4G, and members' entry codes stay readable offline in their app.

Is my data safe?

GMS runs on Amazon Web Services with encrypted storage and 14 days of backups. Each gym only ever sees its own data, and you can export your members to Excel at any time.

Do I need new hardware?

No. A computer, tablet or phone is enough. A USB QR scanner (about $25) makes the entrance faster.

Can I keep using my WhatsApp?

Yes. By default GMS prepares the messages and opens them in your WhatsApp. Elite sends them automatically through WhatsApp Business.
