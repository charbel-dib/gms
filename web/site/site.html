Questions gym owners ask

Do my members need an app?

No. Reception checks them in by name or member number. If you want the app, members open a link and add it to their home screen, without the App Store or Play Store.

We have hundreds of members in Excel. Do we start from zero?

No. Send us your file; we import names, phones, plans, end dates and balances before you start.

What if the internet goes down at the gym?

Check-in keeps working: the desk’s device keeps a copy of your member list, saves each entry and sends it when the connection is back. Payments wait until you are online again, because each one needs its receipt number.

Can my receptionist see the gym’s money?

Only what reception needs: check-in, payments they take and the cash of the day. Profit, reports and settings stay with you and your manager.

My receptionist is not comfortable with computers. Will it work?

The desk has two jobs: type a name to check someone in, and record a payment. Both work on a phone, in Arabic or English, and we train your team on site.

How do members get reminders?

On WhatsApp and as app notifications: before their membership ends, before a booked class, when a waitlist spot opens. On Starter and Pro, reception sends prepared WhatsApp messages in one tap; on Elite they go out on their own.

Is our data safe?

Each gym’s data is separate, every change is logged, connections are encrypted and the database is backed up daily on Amazon Web Services.

Can we stop anytime?

Yes. It’s monthly, and you can export your members to Excel whenever you want.
