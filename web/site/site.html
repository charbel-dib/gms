Questions

My receptionist isn’t good with computers.

The desk does two things: type a name, record a payment. It works on a phone, in Arabic or English, and we show them how at your gym.

What if the internet goes down?

Check-in keeps working on the desk’s device and catches up when the connection is back. Write payments down and record them after.

Do my members have to install something?

No. The desk finds them by name. If you want, members can add your gym’s app to their phone from a link.

Can I stop, and keep my data?

Yes. It’s month to month, and you can export your members to Excel any day.
